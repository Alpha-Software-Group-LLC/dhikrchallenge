name: Product trust guardrails
description: Durable boundaries for progress, private circles, and curated Islamic guidance.
---

Describe progress only as activity inside the app, never as spiritual status. Do not add visible XP, ranks, streak pressure, leaderboards, exact circle counts, scores, or last-active timestamps. Circles may show only whether a member participated that day.

Ask must stay citation-first and limited to the curated library. It must refuse personal rulings, avoid presenting itself as a scholar, and keep school-aware fiqh boundaries explicit.

**Why:** The product is intended to support calm, reflective worship without gamifying spiritual standing or overstating religious authority.

**How to apply:** Use these constraints whenever changing Today, Journey, Circles, completion feedback, Library, or Ask.
